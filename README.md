PROJECT IDEA1: 
AgriBridge KE: Farm to Kitchen Market Volatility & B2B Margins Optimizer 

Problem Statement

SMEs B2B food businesses that rely heavily on fresh produce such as green grocers, hotels & restaurants in Kenya suffer severe seasonal price volatility from unpredictable price spikes as a result of harvest cycles and supply bottlenecks.

Solution
AgriBridge KE. tool connects & tracks farm price signals with retail operations to flag early supply shocks thereby protecting B2B profit margins.





PROJECT IDEA2: 
SMEs MPESA CASHFLOW ANALYZER

Problem Statement
Majority of Kenyan SMEs mix personal and business transactions making it difficult to track profitability in their businesses.

Solution
A cashflow tracker that categorizes expenses and flags unusual spending spikes.
